# Health

The owner's health tracker at `/wizard-health/`. It reads every wearable connector app on the box (WHOOP, Oura, Fitbit, Polar, Withings or any other) through the `health-data` contract and keeps its own copy, so its tools answer fast. Tools: `mcp__wizard__wizard_health__<tool>`.

- "How did I sleep / what's my recovery today / am I ready to train": `health_daily_summary` (omit `day` for the latest). It carries the screen's one traffic light (green good, yellow okay, red needs attention): `recovery_colour`, `sleep_band` (`colour`, `word`, `pct_of_need`) and `activity_match` (`colour`, `word`, `target`); `health_days` rows carry the same fields. Readiness is scored after waking, so early in the day the summary reports the latest scored day (`readiness_date`); say which day it is.
- Specific dates or weeks: `health_days` with `from`/`to` (YYYY-MM-DD, inclusive). Sleep only: `health_sleep`. Workouts: `health_workouts` (sport, minutes, load, HR, calories, zones).
- "How am I trending": `health_trends` with `days` 7, 30 or 90 (averages, change vs the previous period, high/medium/low readiness counts).
- Use the source's own words from `labels`: WHOOP calls readiness "Recovery", sleep score "Sleep performance" and the day's load "Strain" (0-21). Bands: high 67-100, medium 34-66, low 0-33 (WHOOP green/yellow/red). HRV is RMSSD in ms; skin temperature in Celsius; sleep hours exclude awake time.
- Say how fresh the data is (`last_sync` from the source) and don't give medical diagnoses. If a value is missing, say the wearable has not scored it yet.
- Connection or setup questions ("connect my WHOOP", "it says sign in again", "add my Oura"): call `health_status`, then send the owner the connector app link it lists, e.g. `[WHOOP](/wizard-whoop/)`, and say that app's own screen walks him through it. Never walk him through a provider's developer dashboard in chat, and never ask for or accept keys, passwords, sign-in codes or callback addresses in chat. Health itself never takes keys.
- If Health shows nothing new but the wearable synced, the dot in the screen's top corner opens Sources, which has Refresh; `health_status` shows each source's `last_sync` and Health's `last_pull`.
- The screen shows Activity, Recovery and Sleep: in a wide window one row with Activity (the thing to act on) half the width and Recovery and Sleep (the supporting data) a quarter each (a tapped day shows in all three); in a narrow one a two-button bar at the bottom, Activity | Recovery & sleep (Sleep stacked under Recovery), always opening on Activity. Each is a title ("Recovery · how ready your body is", "Sleep · did you get enough", "Activity · did it match your recovery"; only Recovery adds "Measured from your heart overnight, so it can differ from your sleep"), one summary card for the selected day filled with the same colour as that day's square (big number, a word and one short sentence, with 2-4 supporting numbers baked in: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, blood oxygen and skin temperature vs usual; sleep stages and time in bed; calories, steps and workouts; on a phone one tap on the card unfolds them), then a GitHub-style graph of every day in that metric, all three in the same green/yellow/red with one shared legend ("Green good · Yellow okay · Red needs attention", grey No data). The date shows only when a past day is picked, with a Back to today pill. When sleep was Short but recovery is So-so/Ready (or sleep was Enough but recovery is So-so/Run down), the Recovery card adds one short sentence explaining the difference ("Short sleep, but your heart still looks rested." / "Your heart coped, but short sleep is holding this back."). Sleep need can be far above a normal night because the wearable adds catch-up for earlier short nights: when the source splits it (`sleep.need_parts`: `usual_h`, `debt_h`, `strain_h`, `naps_h`), the Sleep card says so ("3h 23m short: 1h 16m under a normal night, plus 2h 7m to catch up from earlier nights.") and its details start with "Your need · usual 7h 36m + 2h 7m catch-up". Explain it to the owner the same way. Colours and words: Recovery green **Ready** 67+ / yellow **So-so** 34-66 / red **Run down** <34. Sleep against need (hours when need is missing: 7h+/6-7h/<6h): green **Enough** 85%+, yellow **A bit short** 70-85%, red **Short** under 70%. **Activity** (the screen's word for WHOOP Strain, 0-21) is judged against that day's recovery: target Ready 14-18, So-so 10-14, Run down under 10; green **Right amount** inside it (or any rest on a Run down day), yellow **Room to push** below it or **A bit much** up to 3 above, red **Too much for today** more than 3 above; less is never red; no recovery score = grey. The Activity card adds a bar (the green target zone on the 0-21 scale, a marker for the day's strain) and one direction sentence: "11.5 to go to reach your target.", "In your target. Fine to stop, or go up to 18.", "1.5 over your target. Take it easy." (more than 3 over: "Stop for today."), "Rest is right today. Stay under 10." on a Run down day. When talking to the owner, prefer the screen's words and plain sentences ("4h 23m less than you needed"); say "heart rate variability (HRV), higher usually means more recovered" rather than bare HRV; tool fields still say `load`/strain.
- Several wearables: answers come from the owner's **main** source (`source`/`source_name`; he picks it in the screen's Sources sheet when two or more are connected; default WHOOP, Oura, Fitbit, Polar, Withings order). A metric the main source lacked that day is filled from another and listed in `filled`, with `origins` (metric -> source id) and `source_names`; say so ("sleep from Oura"). Pass `source` to read one wearable alone. A wearable that is disconnected, not set up or uninstalled stops counting at once (its `days` reads 0, `held_days` and `purge_at` say what Health still holds; it is deleted after 24 h off and read again when it comes back). `no_readiness_from` names a wearable that gives no recovery score: say it has none, never estimate one. Activity targets use each source's own scale (`activity_match.target`, `scale_max`).
- Other apps on the box read the same daily summary at `http://wizard-health:8826/api/summary` (scripts: `http://127.0.0.1:8826/api/summary`). The Morning Report's Health section comes from `GET /api/report?date=&tz=` (format `wizard.report-section/1`); its `final` turns true once last night is scored and ended at least 1.5 h ago, which is the report's wake signal.
