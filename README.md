# Health

A Wizard OS health tracker over the wearables you connect. One traffic light for everything: green good, yellow okay, red needs attention, grey no data, the same three colours in all three graphs and cards with one shared legend. Recovery: Ready 67-100 / So-so 34-66 / Run down 0-33. Sleep: 85%+ of the night's need / 70-85% / under 70% (by hours when the need is missing: 7h+ / 6-7h / under 6h). Activity (0-21, WHOOP's Strain) is judged against that day's recovery, with WHOOP's guidance as the target (Ready 14-18, So-so 10-14, Run down under 10): inside it, or resting on a run-down day, green; below it, or up to 3 above, yellow; more than 3 above, red; no recovery score, grey. The rules live in `web/words.js` (screen) and `app/server.py` (`sleep_band`, `activity_match`, the same fields in `/api/summary`, `/api/days` and the MCP tools).

Recovery, Sleep and Activity each get a title that says what it is and one summary card for the selected day, filled with the same colour as that day's square in the graph (the card wears the square's class, and its text is the kit's ink or paper colour, whichever contrasts more with the fill): a big number, a word (Ready/So-so/Run down; Enough/A bit short/Short against need; Right amount/Room to push/A bit much/Too much for today against that day's recovery), one short sentence (plus a short one when sleep and recovery disagree) and the 2-4 supporting numbers baked in (on a phone one tap on the card unfolds them). Below, every day as a square like GitHub's contribution graph (weeks as columns, newest on the right, only tracked weeks; thumb-sized on a phone and scrolling sideways for older weeks). A wide window (680 px of app width or more) shows one row, Activity half the width and Recovery and Sleep a quarter each, all in sync; a narrow one shows one at a time with a two-button Activity | Recovery & sleep bar at the bottom for the thumb, always opening on Activity. The Activity card draws a bar with the green target zone and a marker for the day's effort, plus one direction line ("11.5 to go to reach your target." / "1.5 over your target. Take it easy."). Tap a square to see that day in every card and graph; the date and a Back to today pill show only then. Back folds an unfolded card, then returns to today. Sources open from the corner dot. On Wizards that offer it, **Reply** (top row, beside the sources dot) opens an unsent Chats draft with the shown day attached (activity, workouts, recovery, sleep with times and stages), so the owner can ask his agent about it; Health itself never sends anything. The launcher icon is `web/icon.svg` (a silhouette the shell tints); `icon.png`/`icon.svg` are the full-colour store icon.

Health never talks to a provider and never takes keys. It reads connector apps that fill the `health-data` spot through the contract [`wizard.health-data/1`](docs/health-data-contract.md), the same split as Money over SimpleFIN/Monarch/Teller:

- [WHOOP](https://github.com/oscarhenrycollins/wizard-whoop) (`wizard-whoop`, 8824), and Oura, Fitbit, Polar and Withings connectors (`wizard-oura` 8828, `wizard-fitbit` 8830, `wizard-polar` 8832, `wizard-withings` 8834). Any connector only has to provide `health-data` and answer the contract; Health finds every one through the App Manager's grants file (`provider_endpoints_all`), `HEALTH_SOURCES`, or its built-in list, and the connector names itself.
- Several sources: the Sources sheet shows each one's state (connected, behind, not set up with a Set up link to its own app). With two or more connected the owner picks the main source (saved server-side; default WHOOP, Oura, Fitbit, Polar, Withings order). Each day uses the main source's record; a metric it lacks that day is filled from another source and marked (`origins`, `filled`, a small "from Oura" note on the card).
- Activity targets come from the recovery band as fractions of the source's `load.scale_max` (WHOOP 0-21 keeps 14-18 / 10-14 / under 10), or the source's own `load.targets`. A source with no readiness score shows "No score from <source>" (grey), never a made-up score.
- When nothing is connected, the screen lists the connector apps and opens them ("Set up Oura" opens `/wizard-oura/`).
- Health keeps its own copy of the days it read (pulled with each source's `changed_since` cursor; dropped and re-read when a source's `dataset` id changes), so screens and agent tools never wait on a connector.

## No wearable: log by hand or import a file (switch `manual-log`, off by default)

With the app switch `wizard-health:manual-log` on, Health is useful with no wearable at all. **Log a day** (on the empty screen, the **Log** button in the top row, or under Sources) is one form: the day, hours asleep, how you feel (Ready / So-so / Run down), active minutes, weight (kg) and a note; any one is enough and saving a day again replaces it. **Import a file** reads a CSV with a header row and one row per day: a `date` column (YYYY-MM-DD) and any of `sleep_hours` (or `sleep`, `7:30` works), `feel`, `activity_minutes` (or `active_minutes`), `weight_kg` (or `weight_lb`), `note`; commas, semicolons or tabs; a date imported again replaces its row; bad rows are skipped and counted. These days are two local sources, **Logged by hand** (`health-log`) and **Imported file** (`health-import`), and fill the same squares, cards and colours: how you feel stands in for recovery (shown as the word, never a percentage; `readiness.felt`), sleep is judged by hours (7h+ / 6-7h / under 6h), active minutes against how you felt (Ready 45-90, So-so 20-45, Run down under 20). A connected wearable wins each metric on a day it covers (hand-logged beats imported); what it lacks (weight, a note) is filled from the log and marked "Logged by hand". The local days are never aged out with a wearable; with the switch off they are hidden, not deleted. **Import a file** also takes the iPhone Health app's export (Health > your picture > Export All Health Data, `export.zip`, or the `export.xml` inside it; #9): the page sends the file as it is, Health spools it to disk under `DATA_DIR` (never memory: the container has 128 MB) and reads `export.xml` straight out of the zip with a streaming parser in the background, keeping per day the time asleep (sleep records marked asleep, a phone and a watch overlapping counted once, on the day you woke up), Apple's exercise minutes (the device with the most that day) and the day's last weight. A 476 MB export.xml (2.1 million records) read in about 10 s with 44 MB peak memory. A day with active minutes but no feeling (such as every Apple Health day) is judged against a daily goal of 30 active minutes: green at 30 or more, yellow below, never red.

## Interfaces

- Screen: `/wizard-health/`
- HTTP API (see `openapi.json`): `GET /api/status`, `/api/summary[?day=&source=]` (daily summary for other apps such as a morning report), `/api/days`, `/api/trends?days=7|30|90`, `/api/workouts`, `/api/sleep`, `/api/wake?date=&tz=` (the wake signal: `woke_at` = last night's sleep end from the wearable, `night_final`, `final_at`), `/api/report?date=&tz=` (its Morning Report section in the shared `wizard.report-section/1` format: the Recovery and Sleep card, a Monday "Last 7 nights" look, and `final`/`night_final` once last night has settled, i.e. scored, ended at least `HEALTH_WAKE_SETTLE_MIN` = 30 min ago (`HEALTH_REPORT_SETTLE_H` still overrides) and the source synced again since, plus `woke_at` and a top-level `wake` object; spec: oscarhenrycollins/morning-report `docs/report-section.md`); `POST /api/refresh`, `/api/primary`, `/api/forget`. With `manual-log` on: `GET /api/log?date=` (that day's hand-logged record), `POST /api/log` (`date`, `sleep_h`, `feel`, `activity_min`, `weight_kg`/`weight_lb`, `note`), `POST /api/log/delete` (`date`, or `source: health-import` for every imported day), `POST /api/import` (`csv` text, `name`; up to `HEALTH_MAX_IMPORT_BYTES`, 4 MB), `POST /api/import/apple-health?name=` (the export.zip or export.xml as the raw body, `Content-Type: application/zip`; up to `HEALTH_MAX_APPLE_BYTES`, 4 GB; 202, then poll `GET /api/import/apple-health` for `state` receiving/reading/done/error, `read`/`total` bytes, `imported`, `from`, `to`, `counts`).
- MCP (`POST /mcp`, box-only): `health_daily_summary`, `health_days`, `health_trends`, `health_sleep`, `health_workouts`, `health_status`, `health_wake`; with `manual-log` on also `health_log_day` (a write tool: it reaches an agent only when the owner ticked Health for it).

## Development

`python3 -m unittest discover -s tests` runs the tests against two fake connectors. `python3 tools/package.py` refreshes the inline Runtipi payload; `python3 tools/package.py --check` verifies it.
